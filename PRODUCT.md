# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Solo bloggers and writers who maintain their own WordPress site. They write frequently and want a distraction-free, native Mac experience that stays out of the way. They know WordPress but don't want to live in the browser admin.

## Product Purpose

Quill is a native macOS editor for WordPress. It replaces the browser-based block editor with a focused writing environment: draft, edit, and publish posts and pages without touching a browser. Success means the writer stays in flow — one keystroke to publish, zero friction in the editor.

## Brand Personality

Clean, efficient, professional. The tool is confident without being loud. Warm enough to suit a writer's workspace, precise enough to feel well-made. No chrome, no clutter — every pixel earns its place.

## Anti-references

- Generic macOS system-blue app feel (looks like Settings or Mail — not distinctive)
- Notion's over-styled dark mode with neon accents
- Heavy "SaaS dashboard" chrome: sidebars with icons, breadcrumbs, status bars everywhere
- Anything that competes with the writing surface for attention

## Design Principles

1. **Writing surface first.** The editor is the product. All chrome is subordinate — visible only when needed.
2. **Native, not web-ported.** Feel like Apple built it. Match macOS conventions for menus, popovers, and system typography where possible.
3. **Warm amber accent, not system blue.** The amber (`#b45309`) is the one distinctive color. It should be consistent across every interactive state: focus rings, active buttons, primary actions.
4. **Quiet until you need it.** UI surfaces (find bar, embed picker, link popover) slide in and out without disrupting the reading line.
5. **Every UI panel speaks the same language.** Consistent input style, button style, and spacing across the in-editor panels — they're all part of the same tool.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Focus rings must be visible and use the amber accent. Reduced motion should be respected for any panel animations.
