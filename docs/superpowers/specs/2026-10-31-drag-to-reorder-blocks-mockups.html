Packing for the coast

The forecast said rain for three days and sun for one, which on that stretch of coast means you pack for all four seasons and wear most of them at once.

I started with a list on the back of a receipt. By the time I reached the car it had grown to both sides and the margins.

What I'd leave behind

The second pair of boots. The novel I never opened. Half the chargers, all of the snacks that need a fridge.

Take half the clothes and twice the money.

What I'd bring again

A thermos, a paper map, and a wool hat that has outlived two jackets.

Getting there

The last forty miles are a single lane with passing places. Leave earlier than you think, and let the locals by.

  1. The receipt was for a thermos.
