Why Pet Care Keeps Growing

Spending on pets has climbed for a decade, and analysts expect that to continue. According to Grand View Research, the market is on a steady upward path.Premium food is the fastest-growing category.

What's Driving Demand

  • Premium products, including:

    • Supplements
  • Growth in pet-tech devices
